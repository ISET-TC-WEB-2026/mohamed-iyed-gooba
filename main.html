histroy of lighting

Lighting has been a part of human life for thousands of years. The earliest forms of lighting were fire-based, such as torches and candles. Over time, humans developed more advanced lighting technologies, including gas lamps and electric lights. Today, lighting is an essential part of modern life, providing illumination for homes, businesses, and public spaces.
